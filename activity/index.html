Beyond the Lab: Creativity and Community Engagement

Although my professional focus lies in molecular biology and computational science, I believe a fulfilling life extends beyond research. My involvement in the arts, education, and community activities provides not only a creative outlet but also strengthens essential skills in communication, leadership, and collaboration that are valuable in every sphere.

Stage & Performance

Stage performance has long been a meaningful part of my journey, offering a platform to express creativity and engage with audiences. From acting in plays to participating in dance, these experiences have sharpened my public speaking abilities and built my confidence.



Couple Dance

Couple dance blends rhythm, movement, and connection into a unique form of artistic expression. In this performance, my partner and I highlight the joy and harmony of this style. Beyond choreography, it reflects chemistry, trust, and communication between dancers, creating an engaging experience for viewers.



Through couple dance, I have learned the value of teamwork and mutual trust. It has also improved my coordination and sense of rhythm—skills that resonate not only in performance but also in scientific practice. I hope you enjoy watching this as much as we enjoyed performing it.



Puthi Recitation

Puthi recitation is a traditional art form rooted in storytelling and cultural preservation. It involves reciting poetic texts, often accompanied by music, to share narratives across generations. In this performance, I present a Puthi recitation that emphasizes not only the words but also the emotion and audience connection that bring stories to life.



Engaging in Puthi recitation has deepened my appreciation for cultural heritage and the art of storytelling. It has also strengthened my public speaking skills, enabling me to connect with audiences in meaningful ways. I hope this performance inspires you to explore the storytelling traditions within your own culture.


Drama Performance

Drama offers a powerful medium for exploring stories and perspectives. My participation in drama has taught me empathy, creativity, and the ability to communicate effectively with audiences on an emotional level.



In this video, I took part in a comedic drama that spoofs an advertisement, showcasing our creativity and timing. The performance is a playful take on marketing, highlighting its quirks and exaggerations.

This experience was both fun and collaborative, allowing me to explore humor in performance art while working closely with others to bring a shared vision to life. I hope you enjoy the creativity and lightheartedness of this piece.

Visual Arts & Drawing

Art has always been a universal language—one that crosses borders and brings people together. My enthusiasm for drawing and visual arts has given me the chance to explore creativity while sharing my perspective with others. In this section, I highlight some of my drawing tutorials, where I demonstrate techniques and provide guidance for aspiring artists.

For me, drawing is more than producing images—it’s about conveying emotions, telling stories, and giving shape to ideas. Through these tutorials, I hope to encourage others to pick up a pencil and begin their own creative journey.

Education & Mentorship

I am deeply committed to teaching and making complex scientific concepts easier to grasp. This video features one of my chemistry tutorials, where I simplify core topics to help students build a strong foundation in science.



In this tutorial, I explain essential chemistry concepts with clear examples and engaging visuals, making the subject more approachable. My aim is to remove barriers to learning and spark curiosity in students.

My teaching philosophy emphasizes clarity, interaction, and real-world application. Education, to me, is not only about transferring knowledge but also about inspiring curiosity and a lifelong love of learning. Through my tutorials, I strive to make science both accessible and enjoyable.

Gaming & Community Moderation

Serving as a community moderator for a video game gave me valuable experience in communication, conflict resolution, and community building. I also enjoy producing helpful content, such as this tutorial video, to support players and encourage a collaborative gaming culture.



In this video, I share strategies and tips for a popular game, helping players sharpen their skills and enjoy the experience more fully. My goal is to create a supportive space where players can learn from one another and grow together.

Moderating a gaming community has shown me the importance of fostering a safe and positive environment. I aim to create content that not only entertains but also educates and empowers players to reach their potential in the gaming world.

Acknowledgments
