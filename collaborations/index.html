Trial Completed

Effectiveness of Calcium Channel Blockade for Organophosphorus and Carbamate Pesticide Poisoning (CCBOC): an Open, Pragmatic, 3-Arm Randomised Controlled Trial

Sponsor: The University of Edinburgh  |  Funder: UK Medical Research Council (MRC), Joint Global Health Trials scheme  |  Chief Investigator: Prof Michael Eddleston, University of Edinburgh

Design: Open-label, pragmatic, 3-arm, phase III RCT — standard care vs. + magnesium sulfate vs. + nimodipine  |  Target enrolment: 3,243 patients  |  Sites: A multi-centre network of hospitals across Bangladesh, coordinated by the Toxicology Society of Bangladesh  |  Registration: ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03925025

Pesticide self-poisoning with organophosphorus and carbamate compounds is the second most common method of suicide worldwide, killing an estimated 150,000 people a year, mostly across rural Asia — yet no new treatment has reached routine practice in five decades. This pragmatic trial tests two widely available, repurposed medicines — the calcium-channel blocker nimodipine and magnesium sulfate — against standard care to determine whether either reduces in-hospital mortality or the need for and duration of mechanical ventilation. Enrolment and data collection are now complete, and I contributed to the trial's data management infrastructure as part of my role at the Toxicology Society of Bangladesh.

Manuscript in Preparation

Clinical Outcome of Acute Organophosphorus/Pyrethroid Combination Self-Poisoning: A Comparative Analysis of Chlorpyrifos/Cypermethrin with Chlorpyrifos Alone

Harrison, E., Al Marzan, A., Sayeed, A. A., et al., Eddleston, M., & Chowdhury, F. R. (corresponding)  |  Prospective multi-centre study across 9 Bangladeshi hospitals, nested within the CCBOC trial infrastructure

This study compared 1,240 laboratory-confirmed cases of acute self-poisoning with a mixed chlorpyrifos/cypermethrin insecticide against chlorpyrifos or cypermethrin alone, drawn from 1,728 patients enrolled and 2,572 screened between December 2020 and April 2024. Mixed-agent poisoning was associated with greater acetylcholinesterase inhibition and a longer hospital stay, though in-hospital mortality and complication rates did not differ significantly from chlorpyrifos alone; overall case fatality in the mixed-poisoning group was 9.7%. The manuscript is being finalized for submission.

Project in Detail
Manuscript Submitted

A 25-Locus PCR-RFLP Signature Atlas and Decision Framework for SARS-CoV-2 Mutation Verification

Alghnnam, F., Alkhalifah, R. I., Bohol, M. F. F., Al Marzan, A., et al., & Al-Qahtani, A. A. (corresponding)  |  King Faisal Specialist Hospital & Research Centre, Riyadh

This study developed and validated a 25-locus PCR-RFLP genotyping panel as a cost-effective, gel-based alternative to whole-genome sequencing for SARS-CoV-2 variant and mutation verification, validated against Sanger sequencing on 127 clinical specimens with full concordance. The approach addresses the genomic-surveillance access gap facing resource-limited laboratories. The manuscript has been submitted for publication.

Manuscript Submitted

Determinants of Serum Biotin (Vitamin B7) Concentrations: Influence of Age, Gender, and Season in a Large Population Cohort

Almalki, E. & Alharthi, M. (co-first authors), Abu Alola, M. J., et al., Al Marzan, A., et al.  |  Dr. Sulaiman Al Habib Medical Group & King Faisal Specialist Hospital and Research Centre, Riyadh

A large retrospective cross-sectional study of serum biotin (vitamin B7) concentrations in 11,735 adults, examining associations with age, gender, and season. Suboptimal biotin concentrations were found in over 40% of the cohort, with female gender associated with significantly higher odds of elevated biotin. The manuscript has been submitted for publication.
