      >
        <h2>About this tool</h2>
        <p>
          This tool predicts miRNA binding affinity to RNA targets and estimates competitive effects of optional competitor molecules. It fuses sequence features with optional 3D structure-derived features, and is trained on curated datasets with experimental binding information.
        </p>
        <br>
        <h3>How it works</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Input your miRNA(s) as multi‑FASTA, plus a single target sequence (FASTA or raw).</li>
          <li>Optionally provide a competitor sequence (FASTA or raw) and 3D structure files (PDB/mmCIF).</li>
          <li>Advanced options include auto‑trimming long miRNAs to a mature‑like window and lossy AA→NT back‑translation where allowed.</li>
          <li>The backend validates inputs, encodes sequences and structures, and performs RAM‑safe batch model inference.</li>
        </ul>
        <br>
        <h3>Results interpretation</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Baseline affinity: predicted binding strength without competitor.</li>
          <li>Affinity with competitor: predicted binding strength considering the competitor.</li>
          <li>Competitive effect: difference between baseline and competitor conditions.</li>
        </ul>
        <li>
          For best results, ensure that miRNA FASTA headers match any provided miRNA 3D filenames (e.g., <code>&gt;hsa-let-7a-5p</code> → <code>hsa-let-7a-5p.pdb</code>).
        </li>
        <br>
        <h3>Points to be noted</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Predictions are based on training data and may not generalize to all miRNA-target pairs.</li>
          <li>3D structure files must correspond to the provided sequences; mismatches will be rejected.</li>
          <li>Back-translation of amino acid sequences to nucleotide is lossy and may introduce inaccuracies.</li>
          <li>Server resource limits may restrict input sizes and batch processing capabilities.</li>   
        </ul>
